# Claude Duet Setup: The Full Playbook

This is my real Claude Duet setup, packaged so you can copy it with your own context.

> **Brand new to Claude Duet? Open [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md) first.** It gets you running in under an hour with 4 beginner scheduled actions. Unsure what a word means? See the [glossary](guide/00-glossary.md).

It covers the main chat, memory, channels, the Tasks page, and every scheduled action I run. Each scheduled action comes as a fill-in-the-blanks prompt you can paste into your own main chat.

> **VIP members only.** Please don't share this repo, post the link publicly, or copy it into a public site. Keeping it quiet keeps it useful for the people inside the community.

## Who this is for

- You use Claude Duet (or want to) as a chief of staff, not just a chatbot.
- You run a business, a personal brand, client work, or all three.
- You want things to happen on a schedule without babysitting them.
- You want the AI to draft and prepare, while you keep the final yes.

No coding needed. A GitHub account helps for backups (step 7), but it's optional.

## Quick start (60 minutes)

1. Read `guide/01-accounts-and-connectors.md` and connect mail, calendar and Drive.
2. Paste the starter memory from `templates/MEMORY-template.md` into your main chat, filled in (step 2).
3. Create 3 or 4 channels for your big areas (step 3).
4. Turn on the 4 starter scheduled actions from `scheduled-actions/README.md`.
5. Make your tracker doc from `templates/scheduled-tasks-tracker-template.md` (step 5).
6. Live with it for a week. Then add one new scheduled action at a time.

Don't turn on all 24 on day one. Each one costs usage, and each one needs tuning.

## How to use the templates

Every template uses placeholders in double curly braces, like `{{YOUR_NAME}}` or `{{YOUR_TIMEZONE}}`. Replace every one before you paste. A quick way: paste the template into your main chat and say:

```
Here's a scheduled action template. Fill in every {{PLACEHOLDER}} from what you know about me, ask me for anything you can't find, then show me the final prompt and schedule before you create it.
```

## The 5 rules behind this whole setup

1. **Claude drafts. You approve.** Nothing posts, sends or pays without your yes.
2. **Write it down or lose it.** Memory, style guides and logs are how every session learns.
3. **Quiet by default.** Scheduled actions post only when something needs you.
4. **Every routine has a stop condition.** No routine runs forever on autopilot.
5. **Watch your usage.** Lean prompts, smart cadence, and a weekly health check.
