# Step 4: Tasks, threads and how to ask

## The Tasks page

Every real piece of work becomes a task. The Tasks page shows where each one stands:

- **Working:** Claude is on it.
- **Ready for you:** there's something to review or a question only you can answer.
- **Stopped:** blocked or waiting on someone else.
- **Resolved:** closed.

Check the page once or twice a day instead of scrolling the chat. When a task shows "ready to mark as done", look at the result and mark it done yourself. Claude doesn't close your tasks for you.

## Threads do the long work

When you ask for something big, the main chat starts a thread for it. The thread does the research, builds the draft, and reports back with one short result. You can open the thread to follow along or steer it, but you don't have to.

## How to ask so you get great results

**Name the outcome, not the steps.** "Get my podcast guest ready for Monday" beats a list of 10 steps.

**Say what's off limits.** "Draft it, don't send it." "Don't schedule anything." "Don't touch the calendar invite yet."

**Give the standard.** "Make sure everything is 100% accurate." "Subject lines short and to the point." Claude carries these into the work and into the routine if you make one.

**Correct with a rule.** When a draft misses, say why in a sentence. "Weak hooks and not great topics." Then ask Claude to save it: "Remember that for future drafts."

**Ask for options when taste matters.** "Give me 3 options." For visuals and memes, I always ask for 3, and 5 when the first set misses.

## Turn good one-off work into a skill

When a thread nails something you'll do again, ask:

```
Update (or create) a skill for this so future outputs match this quality. Include everything I corrected today.
```

Skills are instruction files Claude loads before a job. Your routines can load them too. My X drafts routine loads my personal brand skill, an anti-slop skill and a critic skill before it writes a word.

## Tapbacks count

A thumbs-up on a yes-or-no question means yes. A thumbs-down tells Claude what to change. A heart means you liked it. A draft only sends when you press its Send button.
