# Step 10: Your first two weeks

Don't turn on 20 routines on day one. Build trust one piece at a time.

## Days 1 to 2: foundation

- Connect the core 5 (step 1)
- Paste your memory template (step 2)
- Create 3 or 4 channels (step 3)
- Make 2 or 3 real asks and correct the results out loud

## Days 3 to 4: rhythm

- Read your morning brief each day. Tell Claude what to add or drop.
- Turn on the Friday week in review
- Create your scheduled tasks tracker doc

## Days 5 to 7: content engine

- Fill in your personal brand skill or style guide
- Turn on the X drafts routine
- Pick drafts daily. Edit out loud. Claude logs every pick.

## Week 2: scale up

- Add call prep (if you take calls)
- Add the performance check once a week of posts is live
- Add one client routine, if you have clients
- Add the daily GitHub backup
- Add the weekly health check

## End of week 2: review

Ask your main chat:

```
Look back at the last two weeks. Which routines produced something I used? Which came up empty or I ignored? What should I turn off, slow down or add? Show me usage-heavy ones first.
```

Then prune. A lean setup that you actually use beats a big one you ignore.

## Signs it's working

- You open the chat less, not more
- Drafts need fewer edits each week
- You stop forgetting replies you owe people
- Your Friday review shows real shipped work
