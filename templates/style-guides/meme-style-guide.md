## Rules

Do:
1. Ship within hours of the news.
2. Use a format most people know. "This needs to be understood by most."
3. Keep the caption short, plain, punchline last.
4. Lead with the angle your audience feels: {{YOUR_AUDIENCE_ANGLE}}.
5. Quote-post the news post when there is one, so the caption needn't explain.

Don't:
1. No hashtags, emoji or explaining the joke.
2. No real person's photo or likeness. Redraw templates in a cartoon style.
3. No sexual, family or tragic angles, even when the news is about that. Find the business or tech angle.
4. No copying another account's captions or combos.
