For newsletters, promos and any email drafted in your name. Pair it with the newsletter scorecard (scheduled action 08): drafting agents read the "Email Patterns" tab first, then this guide.

1. Read the "Email Patterns" tab of {{TRACKER_SHEET}} and follow its "Use when drafting" rows.

- Adds to the subject, doesn't repeat it.

## Body

- {{LENGTH}} words (for example: 400 to 500)
- One idea, one main link, one clear call to action.
- In my voice: {{VOICE_RULES}}
- A P.S. that {{PS_RULE}}

## Sending

- Drafts go into {{EMAIL_PLATFORM}} as drafts. Sending is my call.
- Resend to non-openers {{RESEND_DAYS}} days later with a new subject line, if I ask.

## Personal emails in my name

- Open with "Hi {{NAME}}," on its own line. Point in the first sentence.
- Two to five sentences. Sign off with my first name.
- Never: "I hope this finds you well", "just wanted to", "at your earliest convenience".
