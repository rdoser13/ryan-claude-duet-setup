## Hooks that work

- Open with a named tool, my own setup, or a real result with a number.
- A sharp contradiction of common advice.
- Tool vs tool.
- "What I pay for vs what I build myself."

## Hooks that don't

- Aimed at vague groups ("CEOs", "marketers", "employees").
- "X used to be..." or "The most annoying part of..." setups.
- A question you answer in the next line.
- Small personal chores, unless there's a hard number.

## Formats

- One idea per post.
- Short thread (4 to 6 posts) for step-by-step.
- List posts for stacks and costs.
- Past 280 characters is fine if it's strong.
- Add "graphic: receipt" for cost breakdowns, "graphic: old way vs my way" for swaps.
